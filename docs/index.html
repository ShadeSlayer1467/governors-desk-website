04 / A few useful answers

Before you pull up a chair.

Is this an official KingShot product?

No. The Governor’s Desk is an independent project, with no affiliation or endorsement from KingShot. Automation may violate game rules; the name does not imply permission or account safety.

Can I leave everything unattended?

Some workflows need supervision. Unknown screens, unusual popups and longer recovery scenarios remain limitations. Keep an eye on the activity log and use the pause and recheck controls when needed.

Does routine training use speedups?

Ordinary training maintenance collects completed batches and starts the next batch without spending speedups. Arena is different: attempt purchases and paid refreshes can spend gems.
