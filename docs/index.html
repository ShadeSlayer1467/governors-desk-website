A companion for KingShot

The Governor’s Desk

Your cities. Your schedules. Less routine work.

A Windows desktop automation companion for KingShot players using MuMu Player. Organize city schedules and manage recurring training, Intel and Arena work from one place.

In active development

Some workflows need supervision. Pet Adventures and longer unattended recovery still require live validation. Arena attempt purchases and paid refreshes can spend gems.

This is the starter page for the product website. Installation guidance and release links will be added when ready.
